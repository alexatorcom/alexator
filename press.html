Why ALEXATOR?

ALEXATOR is designed as a continuously expanding music catalogue rather than a traditional artist discography. Every new release becomes a permanent chapter of one evolving collection.

Permanent numbersEvery track keeps its catalogue number forever.
Five-track chaptersEach release adds five new individual stories.
One evolving worldThe catalogue never restarts. It only grows.

Project

ALEXATOR combines Progressive Trance, Festival EDM and Electro Rock into cinematic instrumental music driven by electronic energy and expressive electric guitar.

The project exists to give listeners energy, momentum and a sense of moving forward. Every track carries its own title and story while remaining part of a single numbered catalogue.

ALEXATOR Biography

ALEXATOR is a continuously expanding international instrumental music catalogue. The project is not built around disconnected albums, singles or EPs. Every track receives a permanent catalogue number, an individual title and its own story. New releases arrive in chapters of five tracks, and every chapter continues the same evolving catalogue without restarting. Blending Progressive Trance, Festival EDM and Electro Rock, ALEXATOR creates cinematic, high-energy instrumental music shaped by powerful electronic production and expressive electric guitar. The purpose of the project is simple: to create music that gives people energy, momentum and a sense of forward movement.

Project Overview

ALEXATOR is one unified musical catalogue. Every track has a permanent catalogue number, an individual title and its own story. Releases are published in chapters of five tracks: 001–005, 006–010, 011–015 and onward. The catalogue never restarts. It only grows.
